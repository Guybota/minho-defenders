The year is 1368. Castile is at the gates.

Minho Defenders is a hand-drawn tower defense game set on the River Minho, the old border between Portugal and Castile. Fleets of Castilian boats cross the river to besiege four real fortress towns of the Alto Minho: Valença, Monção, Cerveira and the Forte da Lagarteira in Âncora. Stop them with archers, crossbowmen, trebuchets and a good old cauldron of boiling oil.

It is history with a sense of humour. The invaders are after the towels, the Alvarinho and the sardines, the heroines and monsters come from local legend, and every map is drawn like an old engraved chart.

Best played in portrait on a phone. It also plays on tablets and desktop browsers. Nothing to download.

Features

  • 4 historic maps with bastions, walls, gates and a river crossing. Each one has its own paths and landing spots.
  • 4 tower types with 4 levels each and a choice of two specialisations: fire arrows or longbow, heavy arbalest or repeating crossbow, and more.
  • A river full of boats: rowboats, barges, galliots and huge carracks, each carrying a different mix of troops. A knight takes two seats, a battering ram fills a whole barge, and the later the wave, the bigger the fleet.
  • Gates that fall: rams, archers and bombards batter them down. Close the gates to stun the attackers and rebuild them.
  • Two legendary heroines: the Baker of Aljubarrota and Deu-la-Deu Martins. They block enemies, gain experience up to level 10 and unlock a choice of upgrades at levels 3, 6 and 9.
  • A legend for every town: the Towel Merchant, the Coca dragon, the Lagarteira Octopus and the Giant Stag.
  • Blessings and pacts every three waves, kill streaks that pay extra gold, and weather that changes the battle (night, rain and fog).
  • Campaign, free play, endless mode and three difficulties, from Tourist to Captain Paco.
  • Co-op: two players on the same screen, or online with a room code.
  • Original medieval music and voices, generated in your browser, with separate volume sliders for master, music, effects and voices.
  • Plays in English and European Portuguese.

Controls

Everything works with touch or the mouse. Audio starts after your first tap or click.

  • Build: tap a flag, then tap a tower icon twice (the first tap shows its range, the second builds it).
  • Upgrade, specialise, sell or change target: tap a tower and use its panel.
  • Move the heroine: tap her, then tap a spot on the map. Her special power is in her panel.
  • Powers: open the star button in the bottom-left corner. Arrow rain (then tap where it falls), close the gates, and the bell for extra gold.
  • Next wave: the red button at the bottom (or Space on a keyboard). Calling it early gives bonus gold.
  • Zoom and pan: pinch with two fingers, use the mouse wheel or the + and − buttons, and drag with one finger when zoomed in.
  • Pause, speed, sound and menu: the round buttons at the top.

Your game is saved automatically at the end of every wave.
